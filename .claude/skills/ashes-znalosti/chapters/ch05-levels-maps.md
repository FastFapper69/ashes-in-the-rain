# Chapter 5: Levels & maps

## Core Idea
Maps are generated by `gen/levels.py` into `levels.gd` from chunks laid on painted street/room segments. Chapters are groups of thematically linked maps that tell a story; levels should be long, elaborate and dynamic, harder chapter by chapter.

## Structure
- Prologue (Eddie) → ch I (streets, 1931 laundry/market/alley, TENEMENTS), ch II (docks: pier17, sheds, shipping, quay, SANTA LUCIA, CARGO HOLD, HARBOR MASTER), ch III (metro: 14TH STREET, THE TUNNELS, THE CATACOMBS, THE LAST TRAIN, TRAIN YARD). Interludes between chapters: burning-1939-warehouse **dream** (loops), **1931 memory** (ch II map 6, `memory: true` → `_unreal()`), planned Rosie interlude after ch III. Map cards say "A DREAM"/"A MEMORY" and don't count as cases.
- `street()`: never the same segment twice in a row, else the least used.
- Boss rooms: `boss_room` "club" (Mickey, beer kegs as cover), "office" (Kessler).

## Multi-floor
- `scaffold` chunk: 3 floors × 448.5 px, zig-zag ladders (rope kind `ladder`); `roofs()` → `level["roof"] {x0,x1,y,goal,pics,checks}`; roof walk goes LEFT to a firewall goal.
- Generalised `KITS` ("roof", "mezz": scale/line/stub/y/deco/goal/under). 14TH STREET ends with `stairs_up` + mezzanine at y −1318.
- Facades under roofs (tenrow_a–d) have their cornice at the same height and meet the roof bottom exactly.
- `_cam_rise`, roof checkpoints (`checkpoint_y`), `_ground_cp` keeps the street checkpoint (die below → respawn below), `_swap_boxes` (roof vs street crates), bot climbs (`_bot_ladder`).
- **Camera zooms so only the current floor is in frame**; enemies only fire on Frank's floor (ch07).

## Scale & geometry
- Metro drawings were painted at double scale (doors = 2.5 men) → SEG_SIZE 0.52 (SUB). Check scale against a man's height for every new drawing.
- `TRACKLESS` (pump, arches): no train may pass in their view.
- **Deep views** (`DEEP_SPLIT`: alley, laundry, subway…) **never move or distort** (user 17. 11., rule 24): stretching and the perspective slide (DEEP_PERSPECTIVE, DEEP_SLIDE 0.17) were both rejected - the alley "veers off". A deep view moves with its wall exactly as painted. Extra depth only as a separately drawn far layer moved as a whole (translation, no shear), tried in the TEST LAB first.
- **Joins between spaces** (user 17. 11., rule 25): interior never beside a street/alley drawing with a void; in/out of a building only via a drawn opening onto what is really behind it (street door → sidewalk before the same facade; window → that building's fire escape/ledge/roof, or a fall to the street before that facade). A change of kind (interior/street/alley/roof/underground) goes through a drawn door, gate, stair or the facade dissolve, else a fade. `gen/tests/audit_maps.py` checks it.
- Underground maps: no sky (`_build_depth` returns early); a mirrored darker vault strip above scaled metro art. Mirrored sky copy under the sky so a high jump never shows black.

- **No seams** (user, 16. 11., whole game): a drawing never repeats side by side so that its end wall or window shows halfway along a room or hallway. A room wider than its drawing: the drawing centred, dark at both ends (`_build_room`, gen `_room_src`). A long hallway: left end + a middle without end walls, repeated with every joint on a drawn pipe or post + right end; the middle alternates bare wall and a door, never the same door twice running (`_build_sections`, SEC_STAND_*). Brief new art (Gemini) as ends + a middle that joins.

## Map elements
- `mapfx.gd` flags in levels.py: `el` (elevated train over BACK STREETS, full map, fore layer 0.35× camera), `fog` (PIER 17, shortens sight both ways), `neon_drop` (shoot the chains, sign falls on enemies), `storm` (lightning + white silhouettes), `rock` (ship roll via cam.rotation), `sea` (waves over the quay, spoils aim), `steam` + `rats`, `freight` (shots unheard), `flood` (water rises 150 s, wading −35 %), laundry `sheets` (bullet holes).
- `setpieces.gd`: oncoming train in THE LAST TRAIN (headlight, horn, gust pushes a standing player; vents/crouch cover), crane loads (shoot the rope), searchlights, girder drawing, window fires (FIRES, measured sills).
- `gen/levels.py cover_men`: ~60 % of open street men get a crate/drum 140 px in front.
- Rooms are furnished per seed by `furnish()` (ROOM_CLEAR = painted doors/arches/ladders in drawing px, ROOM_KINDS = what belongs there, men behind the cover). `check_props()` fails the build on: gap < 260 px between props (unless a pile), three alike in a row, a red barrel outside BARREL_OK (yards, warehouses, docks, tunnels, back alleys, hold), a prop in front of a door, two alike side by side in a room. Full rules: skill `mapy`; audits `gen/prop_audit.py`, `gen/foe_audit.py`, `--test=props`.
- Diary pages hang on the wall just before a crate at ground reach – never on a crate or cabinet.

## Anti-patterns
- Invisible walls (prologue end) – replace with something seen (mobsters closing in, blocked alley).
- Enemies across floors; streets repeating the same backdrop.
- Props that don't belong (washing machine on a street) or block the path unfairly.

## Key Takeaways
1. Generate → bot clears all maps → look at `--test=rooms` / `look:x` shots.
2. Every new multi-floor map: floor camera zoom + same-floor fire rule.
3. Scale-check every new drawing against Frank (FIG 1.18).

## Buildings in cross-section ("řez domem", 16. 11., first engine pass)
- Generator chunk `section` (gen/levels.py): 3 storeys of `SEC_H` 700 (man ~420), stairs `SEC_RUN` per storey (494 = the stand-in iron flight's slope; the drawn kit sets the final run), zigzag: ground floor right → stairs → first floor left → stairs → second floor right → ladder → roof kit `troof` (y −2100) back left. Exports `sections [x0, x1, storeys, style]`, `section_h`, `xy_checks [x, y]`, and one room per storey `[x0, x1, "section_<style>", top, bottom]` (rooms with y: `_room_at(x, y)`).
- Trial maps live in `levels.gd SANDBOX` (`--sandbox=N`, played as chapter I, never in the case file). `SANDBOX[0]` = SECTION. Bot: `gen/tests/bots.sh` runs it too.
- main.gd: `_build_sections` (storeys shown only from inside, fading in round him; stand-in art = art/rooms/tenement.png until `art/section/tenement_*.png` exists, `_section_kit()`), `_section_at`, `_tick_sections` (fade + storey checkpoints), `_section_dir` (bot: even storeys right, odd left), camera inside = street zoom with the storey held (`_ground_y`), outer walls in `_block` (only the ground-floor left is a way out), solid storey floors are stepped onto off the top stair (reach 34), no `hud.flash` through a building's door, no rain inside.
- **Stairs in the back wall** (user's choice 16. 11.; side-on flights cannot zigzag – the next storey would walk over them): `stairdoors [x, storey y, to y]` from gen/levels.py (SEC_DOOR_RIGHT/LEFT = where the stand-in drawing paints its stairs). Jump at an opening → move_state "stairs" (`_on_stairdoor`): up = climb the painted flight into the dark, the camera follows `_ground_y`, out of the opening above on the level; down = into the opening on the level, down the flight painted below. The flight painted in each storey's opening always leads UP from it. `_grace` covers the way through.
- Men take the same openings (`_take_stairs`/`_on_stairs`): one at a time, only after Frank has stood 2 s on his storey, never placed within 750 px of an arrival; from above: step sound, camera looks up 120 px (`_steps_above`), bark `comedown`/`goup`.
- Inside a building: floors (just under the boards) and outer walls stop bullets (the street door stays open), `_can_see` false across the wall, men inside hidden until the facade is open, `_nearest_enemy` only his storey.
- Facade dissolve from the door: ink shader, SEC_DISSOLVE 0.6 s. Storeys he is not on SEC_DIM, neighbours SEC_VEIL + black sky behind them.
- **The flat, door kick and window jump** (16. 11.): gen/levels.py `flats [door x, y, door h, window x0, x1, sill, lintel]`; the top storey ends in tenement_flat (art_src 223: partition lintel over an open passage, the outer wall cut through with a window). The shut door (flat_door, edge-on) stops him walking; running/diving/sliding into it kicks it in (`_kick_door`: broken drawing flies in, the man right behind goes down, the rest a beat late, free 1.2 s slow). The door stops sight and bullets while shut. Only in the air through the window (`_through_window`): the glass bursts (flat_glass_broken), 0.5 s slow, the fire escape outside (art_src 222, `tenement_escape.png`, ESC_K 1.0526, floors/ramps style "fescape" – drawn, solid decks, railing stops him at the outer end) up to the roof. Steep iron flights: the step reach scales with the slope.

## The modular system (17. 11., QUEUE §10, research ch15 in `ashes-vyzkum`)
How a new piece or map joins the rest so no break shows - in this order, never skipping a step:
1. **Metrics first.** Every drawing has its row in gen/kit.py METRICS (`gen/tests/metrics_audit.py --write`): roofline,
   pavement, doors. A new drawing is briefed to Gemini with the kit's numbers and a grey template rendered in the game's
   own view (`gen/blender/front_template.py`: hk kit, 3168x1344, 1.52 world px per px, pavement row 1250, party walls
   0-124 / 3044-3168, doors 150x360 px). Gemini follows the doors and edges; it also copies the style reference's
   building - always ask for "a DIFFERENT building, only the style shared" (rule 20, no two pieces alike).
2. **Edge contracts.** gen/kit.py EDGES gives each drawing's visible left/right edge a type (party_wall, girder, timber,
   fence, water, open_lot). street() only joins equal types; the firewall pier stands only between two party walls.
   A new drawing gets its EDGES entry the same day (check the edge sheet by eye).
3. **Thresholds.** A change of kind of space passes a drawn opening: an interior's building front (two doors exactly at
   the room's ends), a house's facade door (cross-section), an alley gate, subway stairs, a dock gate. Until a front is
   drawn, an interior is entered by a dip through black and is seen only from inside (main.gd `_tick_rooms`). A hall drawn in parts (ROOM_PARTS / levels.py LAUNDRY_PARTS, the big laundry 18. 11.): drawings one after another cut on painted columns, each catwalk its own layer (gen/laundry_layers.py, Gemini "same picture without the catwalk" + difference), a front in parts (kit.py "parts", gen/laundry_front.py). Inside, the camera never looks past the room's walls (`_room_cam_x`; a room narrower than the screen is zoomed to fill it) - no black band at its ends (user 18. 11.).
4. **No fight across a door.** levels.py `door_clear` keeps street men DOOR_CLEAR (700) from an interior's doors; the
   audit checks it (research ch15: transitions only without enemies on).
5. **Map as a graph of spaces** (next, §10.4): street -> alley mouth -> yard -> back door -> stairwell -> flat -> window ->
   own fire escape -> roof; each node picks a drawing whose edges/openings fit.
Checks: `gen/tests/audit_maps.py` (rules incl. joins and edges, exit 1), `gen/tests/joins_shots.py` (every interior/house
join from outside and inside, 1560 and 1280), the bots.

Chapter I's street is all hk-kit pieces (17. 11.): hk_* (things to play with), alley_mouth, market_row, church_row,
subway_entrance, warehouse_row (hoist), bluenote_row, and the interiors' fronts. A new piece: Blender template
(front_template.py styles tenement/laundry/warehouse/club/shops/church/warehouse_row; alley_template.py alley/--subway),
Gemini in a NEW chat with hk_tailor as style only ("a DIFFERENT building"; words it paints are legible if short and
spelled out), process_art STREET entry, kit.py PIECES (+ clear, not_beside, hoist), EDGES, main.gd SEGMENT_SIZE,
basis_imports + import, then audit, bots, shots.

**Houses in cross-section sit on street joints** (user 21. 11., rule 15): `seat_sections` in gen/levels.py moves a house (and all of the map after it) so its facade starts exactly where a street drawing starts - the facade never covers half of a drawn door. A new map with a section needs nothing extra; a test map with a walked roof over the house is skipped.

**The mob count is the generator's (audit 22. 11.).** gen/levels.py `thin_to_curve` lays the count main.gd `CURVE` asks for at the normal setting (densest spot first, heavies in proportion, rooms keep half, runners stay), so the layout in levels.gd is what is played; main.gd `_cast` only scales for easy/hard. Change CURVE in main.gd and regenerate levels.gd. `settle()` + audit_maps keep men out of blazes, from under falling beams and low stairs and before the map's end, and pickups out of crates.

## Modular city (user 22. 11., §1 rule 31; plan gen/QUEUE_doma.md §21)
Why the painted-street + cut-house look failed (found 22. 11. on the user's phone shots) and what fixes each:
- **Scale mismatch**: the cut house is 700 px a storey, the street drawings' houses 450-530 (tenrow 0.675, hk 0.8 sizes):
  the house towered over its row with a flat cut top, no roof, no side, no neighbours. Fix: one grid for every building
  in front (storey 700, fixed bay), roof/cornice modules, the painted drawings only far behind.
- **Things hung across planes**: the window led onto a fire escape drawn on a piece of somebody else's wall (art_src 222,
  opaque, its top flight ran up to nothing); the washing line went from that rail to a window of a background facade in
  another plane. Fix: an escape belongs to its own building's wall (side wall: seen edge-on, art_src 346 = edge-on escape
  on green, two landings + stair + drop ladder, decks at rows 524 / 1756, 805 px deep, bottom 2443 of 2528); a line runs
  only between two things in one plane (two facing windows across an alley between two module buildings).
- **Black voids round a house** (veil + black sky) read as broken: removed 22. 11. (black voids stay a style elsewhere).
- **Firewall piers** cover street-drawing joins and rise above the roofs as black columns; never behind an escape.
- **Interim fixes shipped 22. 11.** (stay until the modules replace them): `_through_window` no x mark (the wall stop
  at wx0-60 sat before the old wx0-20 mark: nobody got out; `--test=flatwin`); escape drawing faded right of its decks
  and above the reachable landing (`ESC_FADE_*`, shader `_esc_fade_shader`); `HIGH_WIDE` / `HIGH_LOOK_DOWN`: up high
  outside (not on kit roofs, not in a house/room) the camera pulls back 20 % and looks down so the landing spot shows.
- Godot 4 canvas shader lesson: in `fragment()` COLOR already holds texture x modulate - multiplying by texture(TEXTURE)
  again squares it (the escape went black). Only touch `COLOR.a`.
