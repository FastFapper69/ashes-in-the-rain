## 1. Hard rules (the user gets angry when they are broken)
1. **Look at every visual change yourself before delivering** – zoomed screenshots. No AI tells (six fingers, hands through wood, glyph-soup text → `gen/fix_cut_text.py`). UI: zero `UI BAD` lines and eyes on 1280 **and** 1560 wide shots (skill `ui-papir`).
2. **Geometry matches the painting.** Feet exactly on visible surfaces, no platforms/stairs the painting does not have, straight lines never bend (deep views/alleys never move or distort - rule 24), no code-drawn slabs/lines/polygons as visible art – get a drawing (Gemini) instead.
3. **Logic check for every prop and map**: right size next to a man, belongs to the place, collision in its real shape, nothing blocks the path unfairly, no hard cuts between scenes. Run the automated audit over all maps (bot clears every map) – never repeat a mistake already pointed out.
4. **No enemy fire right after a level start or checkpoint respawn.**
5. **Enemies shoot / throw only at a Frank on their own floor** (`_same_floor`, `_can_see`). Multi-floor maps: camera zooms so only the current floor fills the frame. Every new multi-floor map keeps both.
6. **Every light has a visible source and the source's shape** (neon glow in letter shapes, not a round blob). Painted-in light and reflections react with the light (flickering neon dims its reflection). Lamps only where a real lamp hangs (over a sign or door, bare wall) – never on a window, fire escape or sign. **Inked light cones are rejected** ("looks terrible") – do not bring them back; strong amber cones were also disliked.
7. **Two chats deploy to this repo.** Before any commit/deploy: `git fetch` both clones, merge origin's commits first; `deploy.sh` STOPs by itself. Never copy the tree over a newer origin.
8. **Phone memory is the #1 crash cause** (Safari kills the tab, no JS error). Long audio 22050 Hz mono (ffprobe before commit); never Basis for textures read via `get_image` or for comic panels; new big drawing → `python3 gen/basis_imports.py`. Details: [[ch04-phone-memory|ch04]].
9. **Things the user decided – do not reopen**: figures +18 % (FIG 1.18), post `lift` 0.2, character scale, Sin City blacks (black voids are style - but never round a cut-away house: from inside it and off its window / fire escape the street, the houses beside it and the sky show as they are, user 22. 11.), plain lamp glow, levels/XP instead of stars, HP and damage *bought* not automatic, side gun of the player's choice (revolver or Beretta, one or two; changed by the user 16. 11.) + one big gun (can be taken along into a chapter with few rounds), pickups never auto-equip, bullets do not pass through cover (SPLINTERS instead), prologue Eddie overpowered then weak Frank with a rising curve, Eddie wears a different (short) coat.
10. **Cover and props make sense where they stand** (user, 16. 11.): never three identical things in a row, nothing in front of a painted door/passage/ladder, a man's width between two props, rooms not identical copies, red barrels only where fuel is kept – elsewhere a hazard that belongs to the place (hydrant, falling neon, steam pipe, bar bottles, boiler, chandelier…; user 16. 11.), one or two per map, a man stands by it, only Frank's bullet sets it off; stunts and hazards are added chapter by chapter with the maps (3–4 per chapter, list in the story doc tab "Řez domem a kamera"), each with a two-state drawing and the same quality bar as what exists. Enforced by `check_props()` in gen/levels.py – rules in skill `mapy`.
11. **Enemies talk** (user, 16. 11., "like The Last of Us"): every man has a name, the others shout it when he falls; every new behaviour ships with a bark (barks.gd, English caps, ~24 chars).
12. Story: no plot holes; inspired by Max Payne / Alan Wake / L.A. Noire storytelling but never copying; original characters.
13. **The player always sees that mercy and executions mattered, never as a number** (user, 16. 11.): the first surrender is taught (slowed beat, Frank's line, hint), an execution greys the picture and soots Frank's HUD portrait, a man let go washes some off, testimony pages tuck in behind the portrait, the score card lists names (LET GO / SHOT WITH HIS HANDS UP), and the ash changes play (fewer men give up). [[ch11-ui-hud|Ch11]], [[ch10-story-cutscenes|ch10]].
14. **Story decisions 16. 11.** – do not reopen: two endings only (Snow / Ash; the Rourke choice changes the scene inside them); Rourke carried the kerosene in 1931; Rosie is one of the three Kowalczyk children Patrick Malone carried out; chapter IV boss is Rourke (Vail dies in a cutscene). Maps are routes, not streets: in, up floors, along corridors, onto roofs, down fire escapes – building **cross-section** ("řez domem", the facade dissolves) chosen over cuts between rooms. Plan: claude.ai doc "ASHES IN THE RAIN – Dramaturgie a kapitola IV" ([[ch10-story-cutscenes|ch10]]). **Superseded in part 23. 11.** (user approved the story rework, [[Srdce-a-hlas]]): ONE fire only (Pier 17, 1939) - 1931 is just the memory of a fireman father, no arson, Rourke carried no kerosene; Rourke handed Frank the envelope in 1939 and ordered Eddie killed in 1947; Rosie is Eddie's fiancee and Tommy's sister, not a child from 1931; one diary (no police notebook), Rosie keeps its last page. Still true: two endings only, chapter IV boss is Rourke, cross-sections.
15. **No seams, anywhere** (user, 16. 11.): a room or hallway is never two copies of a drawing side by side – no end wall or window halfway along. Ends + a joining middle, or the drawing centred with dark ends. [[ch05-levels-maps|Ch05]], skill `mapy`. **The end of a storey or room never cuts anything drawn in half** (user 19. 11., the tenement: "the ends must not end like this, half a door cut in the wall") - the end wall falls on plain wall; if a piece has a door there, it is redrawn without it.
16. **Stairs in a building are in the back wall** (user's choice 16. 11.): an opening with the flight going up into the dark; jump there, he climbs it, the camera takes the storey, he comes out of the opening above. Never a side-on flight through a storey's floor (a zigzag route cannot work side-on). Inside a building: floors and outer walls stop bullets, nobody inside is seen from the street until the facade opens, aim/sight only on his own storey. "It must look good and make sense – no nonsense" (user). [[ch05-levels-maps|Ch05]]. The opening shows a dimly lit stairwell (its back wall, a sconce, the flight turning up) - never a flat black hole (user 19. 11.: "the black spot is crap and ugly"; gen/stairwell_fill.py).
17. **Voice/dubbing is done at the very end, all at once** (user, 16. 11.): new barks and lines ship as bubbles only; collect them, do not record piecemeal.
18. **Stunts give slowed time for free** (user, 16. 11.): door kick 1.2 s, a swing on a rope 1 s, a window 0.5 s – the slow meter is not touched (`_free_slow`). Repeating stunts (awning bounce, wall kick, bar, washing line, swing) go through `_stunt_slow(kind, secs)`: the same kind gives it again only after 4 s (audit 22. 11.: bouncing on one awning was slowed time for ever).
19. **Nothing hangs or stands without a reason** (user, 16. 11.): a swing rope hangs from something drawn – a crane in the docks, the warehouse hoist's painted pulley in the streets (`hoist_ropes`, warehouse_c). Never a freestanding rope or a wall strip pasted on the map like a sticker: change the drawing itself (a variant) so it belongs to the background.
20. **Looking good comes first** (user, 16. 11.: "regenerate whatever you need, above all it must look good"): redraw art freely (Gemini) instead of shipping something that repeats, reads as a sticker or does not make sense. No two pieces of a kit share the same building behind them; interactive things are cut into their own layer and stand slightly forward of the wall (parallax + contact shadow) so they read 3D in 2D.
21. **New mechanics are tried in the TEST LAB** (user, 16. 11.): every new thing goes into SANDBOX[1] "TEST LAB" (gen/levels.py, right from the start, few men) and the user is launched there (`--sandbox=1`), never made to play through a campaign map to reach it.
22. **Everything has a source and a ground** (user, 17. 11.): steam or smoke rises out of something drawn (a manhole, a pipe, a vent) - never out of bare pavement; a thing stands on a ground that is drawn (no foreground silhouettes on an empty strip); (22. 11., user: "no black space at the bottom, more work with the foreground" - in the modular city the bottom of the picture is the drawn road under the kerb and the near pavement out of focus, the foreground things stand on that: `_build_road`, pilot in the TEST LAB); a line or rope starts and ends on a drawn anchor (a rail post, a pulley on a window jamb). Extends rule 19.
23. **Check every added object against the picture behind it** (user, 17. 11., after the bar, the window, the washing line): before reporting, look at an in-game screenshot of it and check its perspective/tilt (front elevation vs the drawing's view), its position (on the floor line, against the right wall, not floating in a gap) and its scale against doors and people. Fix it before the user sees it.
24. **A painting is never distorted - alleys and deep views stand still** (user, 17. 11., angry: "alleys still veer off like idiots, I thought we went through this"): first the stretching (14.-16. 11.), then the perspective slide DEEP_PERSPECTIVE/DEEP_SLIDE (17. 11.) were both rejected. A deep view moves with its wall exactly as painted (no shear, no slide, no lift). Depth may only come from a separately drawn layer moved as a whole (pure translation, never a warp) - and only after the user sees it in the TEST LAB. [[ch05-levels-maps|Ch05]].
25. **Spaces join the way they would in a real city** (user, 17. 11.: "I can't go from a window into an open alley"): an interior never stands beside a street or alley drawing with a void between them. You get in and out of a building only through a drawn opening onto the place that is really behind it: the street door onto the sidewalk in front of the SAME facade (cross-section, facade dissolve), a window onto that building's own fire escape, ledge or roof (or a fall to the street in front of that facade) - never into an alley, another building or another scene. Every change of kind (interior/street/alley/roof/underground) passes through a drawn door, gate, stair or the facade - otherwise a fade. Enforced by `gen/tests/audit_maps.py` (QUEUE_doma §9). [[ch05-levels-maps|Ch05]].
26. **Art comes only from Gemini, never from Artlist** (user, 17. 11.: "why generate in Artlist? I told you no"): Artlist is for sound and voice only ([[ch09-audio-voice|ch09]]). If Gemini (Chrome / the PC app) is not reachable, stop and ask - no other paid image generator. [[ch03-art-pipeline|Ch03]].
27. **Everything he can climb or use reads as such** (user, 17. 11.: "the ladder by the awning - you can't tell you can jump onto it ... the same with the balconies you can climb, all these interactive things must have it"): fire escape landings and their drop ladders, awnings, ropes, pipes - every interactive thing is its own layer (Gemini: the drawing without it + the thing on flat green), standing slightly forward of the wall with a cast shadow on it and a cold edge of light, never only painted into the background. Extends rule 20. [[ch05-levels-maps|Ch05]], [[ch08-player-progression|ch08]].
28. **The gunsmith's gun is always ONE gun in one state** (user, 17. 11.: "after the rounds are dumped it does not show the empty cylinder ... closed it is the new cylinder and opened it is the old one again - write this down and watch it"): every view of a gun (closed, cylinder open, magazine out, any upgrade animation) shows each part exactly as it is now - worn or new per part, loaded or empty as the moment says (cases out = empty chambers drawn, rounds in = loaded). A new drawing of a gun state comes in worn AND new, loaded AND empty, registered onto the same frame. Check every act with each part worn and new before shipping. [[ch08-player-progression|Ch08]].
29. **Mobsters fight like players, not robots** (user, 17. 11.: "dumb robots... a long gap between shots... when they shout they'll bust the cover I'd expect them to empty a magazine into it fast... all one-shot, then a headshot makes no sense"): pistol men fire single shots, double taps and now and then salvos of 3-4; a man shooting up a crate empties his gun into it; a body hit never kills a man outright at the start (revolver 1.5 vs mob_hp 3: two body hits, HEAVY LOADS one), only a head shot does. [[ch07-enemies-ai|Ch07]].
30. **Every map means something; every move has a reason in the story** (user, 18. 11.: "why would he just walk into a house and jump out of the window? The maps should always mean something"): a door, a house, a window jump or a run happens because the story drives him there (chased in, cornered, after someone) - never a stunt for its own sake. Spaces stay physically true (in on the ground floor = out on the ground floor). Maps hand over to each other: how one ends is how the next begins. Tell it with in-game comic panels like Max Payne 1 and 2 ([[ch10-story-cutscenes|ch10]], QUEUE §17). [[ch05-levels-maps|Ch05]], [[ch10-story-cutscenes|ch10]].
31. **The city in front is built from building modules on one grid; the painted street drawings go to the back** (user 22. 11.: "the houses with the cut-off roof look terrible ... a modular system for houses and streets, built freely on the map as needed" - approved: "exactly as you recommend"): every building Frank touches (enters, climbs, rides a line from, runs the roof of) is assembled from Gemini modules at ONE scale (storey 700 px, fixed bay width): ground floors, upper floors, the fire escape bay, cornice/roof (walkable), corner/side wall, gaps (alley, lot). Its interior has exactly the facade's dimensions, so every house can open. Escapes, ladders, awnings, washing lines snap to the grid; a washing line only ever joins two things in the SAME plane (window to window across an alley). The old 3168 px street drawings become a dimmed far parallax layer only (scale and joins no longer matter there). Pilot in the TEST LAB first (screenshots 1280/1560 beside the old look), then the chapter I house maps, then the other city streets; docks and subway may stay painted. Problems found and their fixes: [[ch05-levels-maps|ch05]] "Modular city". [[ch05-levels-maps|Ch05]], [[ch03-art-pipeline|ch03]].
32. **Ladders only with the stick up; doors switch at the door** (user 23. 11.: "he grabs the ladder in mid flight by himself, on it he climbs up by himself; from the street the inside should switch where I go in at the door, not 10 m before it"): a ladder is taken only with the stick pushed up (hud.up / the up arrow) - at its foot, or in the air past it; on it the stick up climbs, down slides, nothing holds him still, a jump lets go; the hints say "STICK UP AT THE LADDER". Up a ladder in an open hall the camera climbs with him. An interior opens exactly at its drawn door, and only when he goes in: on the pavement past a door he walks by; the stick up at the door takes him in (as onto a ladder - that push does not also take the ladder beside it); out of the hall he walks through the door (user 23. 11.: "there is no way on out of the works"). [[ch05-levels-maps|Ch05]].
33. **Depth only at points, never free lanes** (user 23. 11.: chose "C" over free 2D movement and over 2-3 lanes - aiming on one plane, frontal facades without vanishing points, the stick's up/down already taken, cut-away interiors): the stick up at a drawn opening takes him INTO it - a street door: up its steps onto the threshold, the door swings in onto a dark hall, he stands in the dark opening (no cream contour), only then seen from close only and covered by the frame; a shot leans him out for 0.7 s (user 23. 11., angry: first version left him on the pavement in front of the steps and called him unseen - "how can they not see him"). Every game effect (unseen, covered, safe) needs the picture to show WHY: check the screenshot as a stranger would before claiming it, a ladder, a door into a hall; later an alley mouth, a shop, a car to hide behind. Any move along, down or a jump takes him out. Cars on the road only as set pieces, not a lane for him. [[ch05-levels-maps|Ch05]]. **24. 11.: the dark doorways (alcoves) are WITHDRAWN** - user: "odd and no use" (main.gd `ALCOVES_ON := false`, code kept); doors into halls and ladders stay.
34. **Back everything up, always** (user 23. 11.: "zálohuj vše, vždy i na ten obsidian"): after any work, `bash gen/backup.sh` (also run by every deploy) - the repo committed and pushed, Claude's memory IS the vault's `pamet/` (a junction onto the memory folder, user 23. 11.: "use Obsidian's memory"; local only: the repo is public, the memory is private - never push it). ## 2. Core mental models
- **The phone is the target, desktop numbers lie.** iPhone uses ~100 MB more textures (full-screen buffers at pixel ratio), the web keeps every loaded pack in the wasm heap, and Web Audio decodes whole streams to PCM. Measure with crumbs/crash reports, not with desktop feel.
- **Paintings are the level; code only adds light, collision and motion on top.** Pixel coordinates of drawings are the contract (BigTex reports painted size even when imported smaller) – never change a drawing's reported size.
- **Layout first on kits, variants swapped in after** – gameplay never moves when art changes (levels.gd non-street lines must stay identical).
- **One deploy = sources to `main` + versioned web build to `gh-pages`**, keeping the previous build so a cached phone page still starts.
- **Bot-cleared maps are the regression test.** Any change to movement, scale, cover or AI → run the bot over all maps.
- **The user judges by looking on his iPhone 17.** A feature is not done until it is seen in a screenshot and works in the web build.
